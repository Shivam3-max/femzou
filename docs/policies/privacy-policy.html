This Privacy Policy explains how FemZou collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you visit femzou.com, place an order, or contact us. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

By using femzou.com or placing an order, you agree to the processing described here. Where the law requires your consent, we ask for it separately, and you can withdraw it at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Contact and delivery details: your name, phone number, email address, and billing and shipping addresses.
  • Order details: the products you buy, order value, payment method (prepaid or cash on delivery), and your order, return and refund history.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by our payment partners. We do not see or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. We receive only confirmation of payment and limited details such as the last four digits of a card or a transaction ID.
  • Account details: if you create an account, your login details and saved addresses.
  • Messages you send us: emails, calls, WhatsApp messages and contact-form messages, including any photos or videos you share for a damaged or incorrect product.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, products added to cart, and similar information collected through cookies and similar tools.

2. Why we use it

  • To process, ship and deliver your orders, including cash-on-delivery verification.
  • To handle cancellations, replacements, returns and refunds.
  • To answer your questions and resolve complaints.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep the website secure.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To send you offers and updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp, only if you have agreed. You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations, such as keeping invoices under GST law.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and checkout;
  • payment partners that process your payment [CONFIRM: e.g. Razorpay, PayU, Shopify Payments];
  • courier and logistics partners that deliver your order [CONFIRM: e.g. Shiprocket, Delhivery];
  • messaging and email tools we use to send order updates and, with your consent, marketing;
  • analytics and advertising services such as Google and Meta, which may use cookies to measure visits and show relevant ads;
  • government authorities, courts or law enforcement, when the law requires it.

Some of these service providers may store data outside India. When they do, we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards and process data only for the purposes above.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart, remember your preferences, keep the site secure, and measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site, such as the cart and checkout, may not work without them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Order and invoice records are kept for the period required by tax and accounting law (currently up to 8 years). Marketing data is deleted when you withdraw consent, unless we must keep a record of your opt-out.

6. How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will inform you and the relevant authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update your personal data;
  • erase your personal data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, for example to marketing messages;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so.

To make a request, contact our Grievance Officer below. We may need to verify your identity first.

8. Children

Our website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
