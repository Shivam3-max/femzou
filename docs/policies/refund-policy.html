We want every FemZou order to reach you in perfect condition. Because our products are skin, hair and personal care items, we cannot take back products that have been opened or used, for hygiene and safety reasons. If something is wrong with your order, though, we will put it right.

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel an order any time before it is shipped. Email or call us with your order number (see "How to reach us" below).
  • Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. If you refuse a cash-on-delivery parcel at the door, we may restrict cash on delivery on your future orders.
  • We may cancel an order if a product is out of stock, the price or product details were listed incorrectly, the delivery address is not serviceable, or we suspect fraud. If you have already paid, we will refund you in full.

2. When we offer a replacement or refund

We will replace the product or refund you if:

  • the product arrives damaged or leaking;
  • you receive the wrong product or a wrong size;
  • a product is missing from your order;
  • the product is expired or has a manufacturing defect.

[CONFIRM: replacement first, refund only if a replacement is unavailable, or the customer's choice of either]

3. What we cannot accept

  • Products that have been opened, used, or had their seal broken, except for the reasons in section 2.
  • Requests because you changed your mind or the product did not suit your skin or hair. We recommend a patch test before first use (see our Terms and Conditions).
  • Requests raised more than 7 days after delivery.
  • Free gifts and samples.

4. How to raise a request

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number.
  2. Send clear photos or a video of the product, the outer box and the shipping label. For a damaged or missing item, an unboxing video helps us resolve your request faster.
  3. We will reply within 2 business days. If we need the product back, we will arrange a reverse pickup at no cost to you. If reverse pickup is not available at your pin code, we will tell you how to send it back and refund the return shipping cost [CONFIRM: up to a limit, e.g. ₹100].

5. How refunds are paid

  • Prepaid orders (card, UPI, net banking, wallet): refunded to the original payment method.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders: refunded by bank transfer or UPI to an account you provide. [CONFIRM: or as store credit, if the customer prefers]
  • Refunds are started within [CONFIRM: e.g. 3] business days of approving your request, or of receiving the returned product. It can then take 5 to 7 business days for the money to reach your account, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • For a cancelled prepaid order, the full amount is refunded. For an approved return or replacement, we refund the product price and any shipping charge you paid on it.
